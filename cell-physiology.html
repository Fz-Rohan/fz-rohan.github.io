Cell Physiology

Cell physiology explores how cells work: how they maintain balance, communicate with one another, move materials, and transform energy to stay alive.

Why it matters in biotechnology

Cells are the operating systems of biotechnology. Understanding their behavior helps explain fermentation, tissue culture, drug delivery, genetic engineering, and many of the measurements used in modern biological research.

  • Membrane transport and cellular signaling
  • Homeostasis, metabolism, and energy production
  • How cells respond to their environment

Current learning focus

Studying the mechanisms that allow cells to sense, respond, adapt, and maintain their internal conditions.
