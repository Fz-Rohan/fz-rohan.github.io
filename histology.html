Histology

Histology is the study of tissues under the microscope. I’m learning how cells are arranged into organized tissues and how microscopic structure helps explain biological function.

Why it matters in biotechnology

Biotechnology often depends on understanding what happens inside tissues: how disease changes structure, how treatments affect cells, and how biological samples can be evaluated. Histology builds the observation skills needed to connect a visible pattern with a biological process.

  • Recognizing cells, tissues, and their organization
  • Connecting microscopic structure with function
  • Developing careful observation and documentation habits

Current learning focus

Building a foundation in tissue types, microscopy, staining, and the structure–function relationship.
