Physics in Biotechnology

Physics gives biotechnology a language for describing forces, motion, energy, diffusion, flow, and measurement—ideas that become useful when biology needs to be understood quantitatively.

Why it matters in biotechnology

Many biological systems are physical systems too. Physics helps explain how molecules diffuse, how cells experience forces, how instruments measure signals, and how processes can be modeled, controlled, and improved.

  • Diffusion, osmosis, and molecular movement
  • Forces, energy, fluid flow, and scale
  • Measurement, uncertainty, and quantitative reasoning

Current learning focus

Learning to recognize the physical principles behind biological observations and translate them into measurable questions.
